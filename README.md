## 👋 About me

I work at the intersection of **geospatial analytics** and **AI automation**, turning complex data and manual workflows into systems that help people decide and ship faster.

My foundation is Earth Observation. A BSc in Geoinformatics and Remote Sensing from Amity University, an MSc in Geoinformation and Earth Observation (Geoinformatics) from Faculty ITC, University of Twente, and a research internship at the German Aerospace Center (DLR) working on 30+ years of Landsat land surface temperature data within the TIMELINE project.

Over the past year, I've expanded into AI automation and generative AI- building with **n8n**, designing prompt frameworks with personas, chain-of-thought reasoning, and structured outputs, and shipping workflows end-to-end.

The thread is the same: whether the input is a satellite image or a job description, the question doesn't change — what can be turned into a system that runs without manual effort, and what does the output need to look like for someone to act on it?

I build **AI automation workflows** that turn manual, repeatable work into systems that run on their own. My prompt frameworks use defined personas, chain-of-thought reasoning, structured JSON outputs, and brand-voice matching with attention to reliability and edge-case handling.

**Recent builds:**

- **AI Research Assistant (n8n)** — Takes a single input (e.g. a job description) and produces tailored downstream outputs end-to-end: company research, customized pointers, and structured prep material.
